# Reader Profile

_Last updated: 2026-04-29 (after 2-year list build)_

## Snapshot

A heavy SFF reader with a serious literary streak and a growing nonfiction
appetite. Reads ~50 books/year; just finished building a curated 2-year
list of 100 books. Brings active editorial sensibility to recommendation
sessions — corrects mistagging, weighs caps, demands specific reasons for
picks, and rejects "tone mismatch" cop-outs without checking the log.

Wants gritty prose, real stakes, mythological/thematic depth, and plots
that actually go somewhere. Beautiful sentences alone won't carry a book —
pacing matters. But the palate is **broad**: warm-sweeping epics
(Wheel of Time, Pratchett, Cahill, Lions of Al-Rassan) sit comfortably
next to grimdark (Abercrombie, Hayes, Wang).

## Positive indicators

- **Mythological / theological texture as worldbuilding.** Christian
  mythology in *Between Two Fires*, religion-as-magic-system in *Blood Over
  Bright Haven*, the ecclesiastical scaffolding of Sun Eater. The deeper
  the system, the better.
- **Gritty, well-crafted prose with earned darkness.** Buehlman, Abercrombie
  (*Best Served Cold* 5.0), Wang, Hayes. Not nihilism for its own sake —
  darkness that means something.
- **Plot twists + thematic weight together.** *Blood Over Bright Haven* hit
  because it had ideas to chew on AND surprises. Books that are smart but
  inert don't land.
- **Epic scope and operatic stakes.** Wheel of Time (multiple 5.0s), LOTR
  5.0, Sun Eater, Red Rising 2–3, *Lions of Al-Rassan* 4.5. When it's
  grand, the reader leans in.
- **Vengeance arcs.** *Best Served Cold* 5.0, Sword of Kaigen, *Never Die*
  pick, *War Eternal* / Eskara appeal, *Stars My Destination* pick.
- **Religious wrestling.** *Canticle for Leibowitz* 4.75; *Mother Night*,
  *East of Eden*, *Lincoln in the Bardo* picked from Lit Fic.
- **Eastern / wuxia flavor.** Sword of Kaigen taken; Never Die taken
  enthusiastically; Asian Saga setting interest.
- **Indie literary credentials.** SPFBO winners (*By Blood, by Salt*),
  sustained r/fantasy hype (*Way of Edan*), Booker comps. Reader knows the
  indie scene and rewards quality.
- **Found family + multi-POV ensembles.** Consistent positive: Jade City,
  The Heroes, Black Company, Lonesome Dove energy.
- **Historical fiction with weight.** *The Nightingale* 5.0, *James* 4.75,
  *Lions of Al-Rassan* 4.5, *Canticle* 4.75. War, faith, displacement.
- **Nonfiction in the cult / disease / strange-Americana vein.**
  *Cultish* 5.0, *Everything is Tuberculosis* 4.5, *Troublemaker* 4.0.
  Curious about how systems and beliefs warp people.
- **Pratchett at his sharpest.** *Guards! Guards!* 4.75 vs *Moving
  Pictures* 3.5 — works when the satire has bite and a proper ensemble.

## Negative indicators

- **Slow plots without payoff.** *Parable of the Sower* 2.75 ("nothing
  interesting happened the whole time"). Wolfe drifting downward
  (3.5–4) for similar reasons.
- **Claustrophobic interiority dressed as epic.** *Interview With the
  Vampire* 2.25 — expected scope, got "petty family drama."
- **Horror that isn't actually horror.** Same Anne Rice problem.
- **Unexamined ick.** Age-gap relationships portrayed without
  interrogation (Parable). Not squeamish about dark content — squeamish
  about *unconsidered* dark content.
- **Progression-fantasy systems-driven.** Cradle rejected; reader is not
  a min-max progression reader despite enjoying Sufficiently Advanced
  Magic. The systems can't be the point.
- **"Humble protagonist destined for greatness" coming-of-age military.**
  Blood Song rejected; Herald flagged for the same risk before being
  taken with eyes open.
- **Slow literary mystery without payoff.** Senlin Ascends rejected
  despite literary credentials — needs more forward propulsion.
- **Genre-blender weirdness without throughline.** Mushroom Blues
  rejected; the genre stack has to feel earned, not collaged.
- **Filler entries in long series.** *Moving Pictures* 3.5 vs City Watch
  peaks — quality varies and the reader notices.

## Format and pacing

- **Audio:** mix freely; flag standout audiobooks with 🎧, otherwise pick
  what fits the book.
- **Series rhythm:** push through one series at a time. Sun Eater is
  effectively complete (only Vol 4 of side tales left); BotNS now
  committed through all 4 books. Discworld, Red Rising, Hyperion Cantos,
  and the new Long Series anchors (Black Company, Farseer, Burning,
  Empire of the Wolf, Last Kingdom, Emperor, Expanse pending) stay in
  rotation but don't dominate.
- **Pace:** ~50 books/year, two-year list = 100 books. Achieved.

## Calibration notes for future librarian sessions

These are the hard-earned course corrections from this build. Apply them.

1. **Don't over-index on grimdark.** Taste is broad. Warm-sweeping epics
   (WoT, Pratchett, Cahill, Lions) land just as hard. Using "warm" or
   "hopeful" as a strike against a pick is wrong.
2. **Check the reading log first.** Before writing off an author or
   series on tone grounds, search the log. Reader has read more than the
   surface signal suggests (e.g. *Murder at Spindle Manor*, full Cosmere,
   Library trilogy).
3. **Use fuzzy catalog matching.** Search by series name, title-only,
   and partial matches — not just exact `Title - Author` keys. *Obsidian
   Path*, *By Blood, by Salt*, and *Realm Breaker* were all in the
   catalog despite my initial "missing" reports.
4. **Trust the reader on category corrections.** Catalog tagging is
   imperfect. Reader-flagged corrections so far: BotNS is SF (not
   Fantasy), Remembrance of Earth's Past is Short Series (not Long),
   Asian Saga is loosely-connected standalones (not Long Series). Defer
   to the reader on classification.
5. **Default to full series when reader commits.** Cahill 4-book run,
   Farseer trilogy, Book of the Ancestor 3, Empire of the Wolf 3, War
   Eternal 5, Children of Time 3 — all taken complete. Black Company
   stayed opener-only as a deliberate exception (and reader stress-tested
   to confirm I'd notice).
6. **Yes-signals to weight high:** SPFBO winners, Booker / Pulitzer /
   Hugo credentials, sustained r/fantasy hype, author already in pocket,
   eastern setting, mythological/religious framing, vengeance arc,
   ensemble cast.
7. **Reader engages with caps actively.** When a pick breaks a target,
   surface the math (which targets bump, which drop) and let the reader
   decide. Don't silently restructure.
