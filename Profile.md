# Reader Profile

_Last updated: 2026-04-29_

## Tone palette — wide

Full warm-to-dark range live. Older 5-stars sit in warm-epic territory (Tolkien, Wheel of Time, Stormlight, Mistborn, Witcher saga, Best Served Cold, Name of the Wind, Words of Radiance). Recent 5-stars lean darker / more literary (Slaughterhouse-Five, Between Two Fires, Sun Eater entries, Morning Star). Don't narrow to one pole — both registers active, both selections in the wish list (Bound and the Broken epic warmth + China Miéville esoterica) confirm the breadth.

## Positive indicators

- **Literary voice carrying dark moral weight.** Vonnegut's anti-war fragmenting, Buehlman's plague-era cosmic horror, Ruocchio's first-person memoir cadence, Cormac-adjacent registers all stack favorably.
- **Cosmic / metaphysical horror.** Between Two Fires, Pet Sematary, Library at Mount Char, Ballad of Black Tom — grim, lyrical, mythic-stakes horror lands hard.
- **Long-arc earned epics with payoff.** Wheel of Time (multiple 5-stars including A Memory of Light), Stormlight (Way of Kings, Words of Radiance, Oathbringer 5-star), Sun Eater (Demon in White, Disquiet Gods, Shadows Upon Time, Dregs of Empire).
- **Grimdark with character interiority.** Best Served Cold (5), Last Argument of Kings, Before They Are Hanged, Black Company territory.
- **Voice-driven nonfiction.** Cultish (5), Empire of Pain, Born a Crime, Maus, Everything is Tuberculosis. Strong rhetorical voice, dark or fanatical subject matter, journalistic rigor.
- **Historical fiction with interior weight.** Lions of Al-Rassan, James, The Nightingale (5).
- **Indie progression-fantasy / LitRPG when craft holds.** Dungeon Crawler Carl saga 4.75–5 throughout, Murder at Spindle Manor 4.5.
- **Witcher-style monster-hunter loops.** Last Wish, Sword of Destiny, Time of Contempt all 5-star — explicit wish for more of this register.

## Negative indicators

- **Didactic message-fiction.** Katabasis (3.25), Parable of the Sower (2.75), Death of the Author (3) — when the moral framing pulls ahead of the story, ratings drop.
- **Mannered / florid voice without substance underneath.** Interview With the Vampire (2.25) — Anne Rice's style felt empty. Distinct from rich literary prose (Vonnegut, Buehlman, Wolfe), which lands.
- **Banks-flavored SF and competent-but-flat space opera.** Consider Phlebas (2.75), The Mercy of Gods (3.0). Distance, sprawl-without-interiority underperforms.
- **Gothic vampire as a register**, based on Interview With the Vampire response. Not a hard rule — Buehlman's monsters work — but mannered immortal romance fails.

1. **Slaughterhouse-Five — Vonnegut** (5, recent). Voice + structural risk + moral weight.
2. **Between Two Fires — Buehlman** (5, recent). Cosmic horror + lyrical grimdark + faith-under-siege.
3. **Empire of Silence / Demon in White / Sun Eater run — Ruocchio** (multiple 5/4.75). Memoir voice + space empire + literary register.
4. **Best Served Cold — Abercrombie** (5). Grimdark with tight character work.
5. **A Memory of Light / Stormlight peaks — Jordan/Sanderson** (5s). Long-arc earned payoff.
