# Reader Profile

_Last updated: 2026-04-29_

## Snapshot

A heavy SFF reader with a serious literary streak and a growing nonfiction
appetite. Wants gritty prose, real stakes, mythological/thematic depth, and
plots that actually go somewhere. Beautiful sentences alone won't carry a
book — pacing matters. Currently 411 finished reads in the log; reads ~50
books a year.

## Positive indicators

- **Mythological / theological texture as worldbuilding.** Christian
  mythology in *Between Two Fires*, religion-as-magic-system in *Blood Over
  Bright Haven*, the ecclesiastical scaffolding of Sun Eater. The deeper
  the system, the better.
- **Gritty, well-crafted prose with earned darkness.** Buehlman, Abercrombie
  (*Best Served Cold* 5.0), Wang. Not nihilism for its own sake — darkness
  that means something.
- **Plot twists + thematic weight together.** *Blood Over Bright Haven* hit
  because it had ideas to chew on AND surprises. Books that are smart but
  inert don't land.
- **Epic scope and operatic stakes.** Wheel of Time (multiple 5.0s), LOTR
  5.0, Sun Eater, Red Rising books 2-3, *The Lions of Al-Rassan* 4.5. When
  it's grand, the reader leans in.
- **Historical fiction with weight.** *The Nightingale* 5.0, *James* 4.75,
  *Lions of Al-Rassan* 4.5, *A Canticle for Leibowitz* 4.75. Strong
  showing.
- **Nonfiction in the cult / disease / strange-Americana vein.**
  *Cultish* 5.0, *Everything is Tuberculosis* 4.5, *Troublemaker* 4.0.
  Curious about how systems and beliefs warp people.
- **Pratchett at his sharpest.** *Guards! Guards!* 4.75 vs *Moving
  Pictures* 3.5 — works best when the satire has bite and a
  proper ensemble (City Watch, Death, Witches subseries).

## Negative indicators

- **Slow plots without payoff.** *Parable of the Sower* 2.75 ("nothing
  interesting happened the whole time"). Wolfe drifting downward (3.5–4)
  for similar reasons.
- **Claustrophobic interiority dressed as epic.** *Interview With the
  Vampire* 2.25 — expected scope, got "petty family drama."
- **Horror that isn't actually horror.** Same Anne Rice problem: the
  premise promises stakes the book doesn't deliver.
- **Unexamined ick.** Age-gap relationships portrayed without
  interrogation (Parable). Not squeamish about dark content — squeamish
  about *unconsidered* dark content.
- **Filler entries in long series.** *Moving Pictures* 3.5 vs City Watch
  books — quality varies and the reader notices.

## Benchmarks

- **5.0:** *Morning Star*, *Between Two Fires*, *Shadows Upon Time*,
  *Slaughterhouse-Five*, *Cultish*, *Blood Over Bright Haven*, *A Drop of
  Corruption*, *The Nightingale*, *Best Served Cold*, *The Lord of the
  Rings*, *A Memory of Light*.
- **4.75:** *Golden Son*, *A Canticle for Leibowitz*, *Isles of the
  Emberdark*, *James*, *Guards! Guards!*.
- **Negative benchmarks:** *Interview With the Vampire* 2.25, *Parable of
  the Sower* 2.75, *Katabasis* 3.25.

## Format and pacing

- **Audio:** mix freely; flag standout audiobooks with 🎧, otherwise pick
  what fits the book.
- **Series rhythm:** push through one series at a time. **Sun Eater is
  the anchor for this list** — keep advancing Ruocchio. Continue Book of
  the New Sun for one more (*Sword of the Lictor*) before deciding.
  Discworld and Red Rising stay in flight but don't dominate.
- **Pace:** ~50 books/year, two-year list = ~100 books.

## Genre lanes (preliminary, finalized in goals)

- **Fantasy / SFF:** the home base. Gritty, mythic, or operatic; not
  cozy.
- **Horror:** confirmed lane after *Between Two Fires* and *Pet
  Sematary*. Wants real horror, not gothic mood pieces.
- **Historical Fiction:** strong lane. War, faith, displacement.
- **Literary Fiction:** selective. Vonnegut works; depends on
  voice and forward motion.
- **Nonfiction:** moderate lane (5–8 picks). Cult/belief, disease,
  Americana strangeness.
- **Crime / Mystery / Thriller:** light coverage in recent log
  (*Malice* 4.0). Open to more if it has texture.
